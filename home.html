College of Information and Computing Sciences

The College of Information and Computing Sciences (CICS) stands at the forefront of technological education and innovation. Our department is committed to nurturing the next generation of tech leaders through cutting-edge curriculum, hands-on projects, and industry partnerships.

College of Informatics and Computing Sciences

Bachelor of Science Information Technology

Explore the diverse specializations within IT

Network Technology

This track focuses on designing, implementing, and managing computer networks that allow data to be exchanged across devices. Students learn about configuring routers and switches, network security, wireless communication, and troubleshooting network issues. Careers include network administrator, network engineer, and systems administrator, responsible for keeping organizational networks reliable, secure, and efficient.

Business Analytics

This major involves the analysis of data to support business decision-making. It covers collecting, processing, and analyzing large datasets to identify trends and insights. Students develop skills in data management, statistics, and business intelligence tools. Graduates typically work as data analysts or business analysts helping companies make data-driven strategic decisions.

Service Management

This track centers on managing IT services and support in organizations, ensuring IT systems meet business needs efficiently. It includes IT project management, service delivery, client relations, and possibly IT consulting. Careers here involve overseeing IT operations, IT service management frameworks, and user support to maintain business productivity.
